    Generated, not recorded

    Every frame is drawn by your browser as you watch, or captured from your camera if you allow it. Both peers live in this tab, so no frame ever leaves your device.

    Real encode, real transport

    Frames run through a GLSL shader, get compressed by your browser's video encoder, and travel as SRTP packets over an ICE-negotiated RTCPeerConnection.

    Real controls

    The bitrate cap is applied live with RTCRtpSender.setParameters(). Codec switches renegotiate SDP on the fly. Every number comes straight from getStats().
